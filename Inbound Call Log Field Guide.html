    Before you start

    Three things to keep in mind

    1

    One call, many rows

    The table and Status Breakdown count events. Total Unique Attempts counts each Call ID once.

    2

    Filters change the result

    A short date range, or an agent or IVR filter, can hide part of a call and change how it's counted.

    3

    Missed is where you start

    A missed label on its own doesn't prove an agent's fault, a technical problem, or who was available.

      For Victoria managers

      Pull your Victoria numbers yourself

      Turn AI Excluded off and leave Agent Name on All. Then read two lines in the Status Breakdown: Forwarded to AI is how many calls went to Victoria, and Forwarded back (Escalated) from AI is how many she sent back to the team.

      Two Call IDs is normal

      An escalated call is two linked calls in the system. Open either one and the history shows both parts in order.
